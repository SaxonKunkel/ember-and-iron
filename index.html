Skirmish

How to play

  • Win by destroying every rival Town Center and all of their villagers — or by keeping every relic on the map in your Monasteries for 4 minutes. Lose if a rival does either to you.
  • Gather: select villagers and right-click sheep, deer, berries, fish, trees, gold or stone. They carry 10 at a time to the nearest Town Center or matching camp (Mill = food, Lumber Camp = wood, Mining Camp = gold & stone).
  • Food: your town starts with a flock of sheep that gathers at the Town Center — the fastest food. Stray sheep join whoever finds them first, and unguarded flocks can be stolen. Deer graze nearby (villagers hunt them with spears), fish swim along lake shores, and farms never run out.
  • Build: with villagers selected, pick a building in the bottom-right (or its hotkey) and left-click to place. Shift-click to place several. More builders finish faster. Right-click a damaged building to repair.
  • Grow: Houses add +5 population. Train villagers at the Town Center, spearmen at the Barracks, archers at the Archery Range. Farms need a Mill and give endless food.
  • Advance: research the Town Age, then the Castle Age, at your Town Center (U). New ages unlock the Stable, Watchtowers, scout riders, knights, battering rams and stronger upgrades. Camps, the Mill and military buildings all offer upgrades.
  • Counters: spearmen beat cavalry, cavalry beat archers, archers beat spearmen. Mangonels crush packed infantry and archers. Rams and trebuchets wreck buildings but cannot fight units; cavalry make short work of all siege.
  • Castle Age: build a Keep (stone) to train your civilization's unique unit and trebuchets, and to research its Veteran upgrade. The Siege Workshop builds rams and mangonels.
  • Monks (Monastery, Town Age) heal nearby wounded units on their own. Right-click an enemy unit to convert it — then the monk needs time to regain faith. Right-click a relic to carry it to your Monastery, where it earns gold.
  • Civilizations: eight real medieval peoples, each with bonuses drawn from what it was known for, a favored army, and a unique unit trained at the Keep. Hover the civ name in the top bar to see yours. Rivals get a random civilization.
  • Fishing: villagers can fish shore fish. A Dock (built in the water against the shore) trains Fishing Ships, which also reach deep-water fish further out and build Fish Traps — endless food on the water. Pick the Lakes or Coastal map type for lots of water.
  • Sea battles (Town Age, at the Dock): the War Galley shoots arrows at ships and at anything near the shore; the Fire Ship burns galleys at close range and shrugs off arrows; the Demolition Ship rams and explodes, wrecking Fire Ships and bunched-up fleets — but galleys sink it before it arrives. Towers and Town Centers shoot ships too.
  • Transports carry 5 units. Select units and right-click (or tap) your Transport to board; then right-click a spot on the shore to sail there and land them, or press Unload. If a Transport sinks, everyone aboard is lost.
  • Walls: pick Stone Wall and drag to draw a line. Place a Gate on one of your walls to let your units through.
  • Fight: right-click an enemy to attack, or press Q and click to attack-move. Your Town Center shoots arrows at raiders.

Playing on a phone or tablet

  • Tap a unit or building to select it. With units selected, tap the ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack, or your damaged building to repair.
  • Drag one finger to scroll. Pinch to zoom; two fingers also scroll.
  • Tap Box (top left), then drag over your units to grab a whole group. Double-tap a unit to select every one of its kind on screen. Army selects all your soldiers, Idle army the ones doing nothing, Town jumps to your Town Center, Clear deselects and cancels a building you were about to place, and Full hides the browser bars.
  • Press and hold any button to see what it costs and does, without pressing it.
  • Tap Hide (above the bottom-right of the panel) to tuck the panel away so the map fills the screen; tap Panel to bring it back.
  • Save files: in the Menu, Export save file sends your game to Files, Messages or AirDrop; Import save file picks it up on another device.
  • Building: pick a building, drag the outline to the spot, then lift your finger. For walls, drag along the line you want.
  • Attack-move: tap the Attack-move button, then tap where to go.
